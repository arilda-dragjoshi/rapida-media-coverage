UNDP RAPIDA Monitor

Privacy notice

What this tool is for

UNDP RAPIDA Monitor helps UNDP's Crisis Bureau see where its UNDP RAPIDA assessments are referenced in the news, on the web and on social media. It collects public references through news feeds and official interfaces (APIs). It does not open or copy web pages, and it does not build profiles of people.

What is collected

  • About public references: the link, headline, outlet, date, platform, language, and the sentence that mentions UNDP RAPIDA. A headline or sentence can contain the name of a person who wrote or was quoted in it.
  • About UNDP colleagues who use the tool: work email address, name, role, when they last signed in, and which references they accepted or rejected.
  • Not collected: what colleagues type in the search box (searches are only counted), visitors' IP addresses, and anything from the public pages' visitors.

How long it is kept

  • Accepted references: the link and the sentence that mentions UNDP RAPIDA, until removed.
  • Rejected references: after 24 hours only the link and its status are kept, so the same item is not collected again.
  • Activity log, run history and backups: a retention schedule is built into the tool and will be switched on once UNDP has approved the periods. [Periods to be confirmed.]

Who can see it

Only UNDP colleagues who have been given access. The public pages and the public data feed show accepted references only; accounts of private individuals are shown as the platform and a link, without the account name.

Cookies and storage in your browser

  • One cookie, strictly necessary (rapida_session): a random number that keeps you signed in for up to 12 hours. It contains no personal data.
  • One preference kept in your browser's local storage (rapida-list-…): how you last sorted and grouped a list. It never leaves your browser.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.

Your rights, and how to ask for removal

You can ask to see, correct or remove information about you: as a user of the tool, or as a person named in a reference shown here. Removal takes the reference out of the tool, the public pages and the data feed.

Contact: [UNDP contact point to be confirmed — Crisis Bureau, Assessments and Digital Solutions team / UNDP data protection focal point]. Please include the link of the page concerned.
